# Service Outages and Planned Maintenance

Queue: Service Outages and Maintenance

## Confirming an outage
Before replying, check the internal status dashboard and the public status page. If an incident is already open, link the customer's ticket to the parent incident so they receive automatic updates. If no incident is open and three or more customers report the same symptom within 30 minutes, open a new incident and page the on-call engineer.

## Communicating during an outage
Acknowledge the impact, share the incident reference, give the next update time (every 60 minutes for P1 incidents), and offer any available workaround. Do not promise a resolution time unless engineering has confirmed it.

## Planned maintenance
Planned maintenance is announced at least 72 hours in advance by e-mail and on the status page. Maintenance windows are scheduled on Sundays between 01:00 and 05:00 UTC. Enterprise customers can request a maintenance-window exception up to 7 days in advance.

## Service credits
Customers on plans with a 99.9% uptime SLA are eligible for service credits if monthly availability falls below the commitment: 10% credit below 99.9%, 25% below 99.0%, and 50% below 95.0%. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident.

## After the incident
A post-incident summary is published within 5 business days for any P1 outage. Agents should share it with affected customers who asked for a root-cause explanation.
