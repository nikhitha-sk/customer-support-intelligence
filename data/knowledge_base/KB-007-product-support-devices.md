# Product Support: Devices, Smart Home and Integrations

Queue: Product Support

## Device setup
Most connection problems during setup are caused by 5 GHz-only Wi-Fi networks; our smart devices require a 2.4 GHz network. Ask the customer to keep the device within 3 metres of the router during setup and to disable VPNs on the phone used for pairing.

## Smart home integrations
Our devices integrate with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant and Apple HomeKit. Alexa and Google Assistant are linked through the companion app under Integrations; HomeKit pairing uses the setup code printed on the device label. Integration capabilities vary by model; check the compatibility matrix before confirming a feature.

## Firmware updates
Firmware updates install automatically overnight when the device is idle. A device that fails to update can be updated manually from the companion app. Do not unplug a device during a firmware update; if this happened and the device is unresponsive, follow the recovery-mode steps (hold the reset button for 15 seconds).

## Hardware issues
For projectors, monitors and printers, ask for the model number and serial number, check cable connections and try a factory reset. If the issue persists and the product is under warranty, arrange a replacement (see the Returns policy).

## Product information requests
For questions about specifications, compatibility or feature roadmaps, share the product data sheet. Do not commit to unreleased features or release dates.
