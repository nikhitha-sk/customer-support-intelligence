# IT Support: Network, VPN, E-mail and Workplace Systems

Queue: IT Support

## VPN connectivity
If the VPN client fails to connect, check that the client version is current, that the user's credentials have not expired, and that the local network does not block UDP port 1194. Users who travel should switch to the TCP fallback profile.

## E-mail and collaboration tools
For mailbox sync issues, remove and re-add the account on the affected device. Mailboxes have a 50 GB quota; users near the quota should archive old mail. Shared mailbox access requests must be approved by the mailbox owner.

## Hardware requests and replacements
Laptop or peripheral requests are raised through the IT service catalogue and require manager approval. Standard delivery time for replacement laptops is 3 business days; loaner devices are available for urgent cases.

## Software installation and licences
Users can install approved software from the self-service portal. Software outside the approved catalogue requires a security review that typically takes 5 business days.

## Network infrastructure changes
Changes to firewalls, routers or shared network configuration follow the change-management process: submit a change request with the business justification, risk assessment and rollback plan at least 5 business days before the desired date. Emergency changes require approval from the on-call IT manager.
