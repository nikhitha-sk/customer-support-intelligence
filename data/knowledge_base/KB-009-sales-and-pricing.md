# Sales, Pricing, Demos and Enterprise Plans

Queue: Sales and Pre-Sales

## Plans and pricing
We offer Starter, Professional and Enterprise plans. Starter and Professional can be bought online, billed monthly or annually (annual billing saves 15%). Enterprise pricing is quoted based on seats, usage and support level.

## Demos and trials
Prospects can start a 14-day free trial of the Professional plan without a credit card. Live demos are booked through the sales calendar; send the booking link and ask about team size, current tools and the main problem they want to solve.

## Volume and education discounts
Discounts start at 50 seats. Registered non-profits and accredited educational institutions are eligible for a 30% discount with proof of status.

## Security and compliance questionnaires
Share the standard security pack (SOC 2 report under NDA, data processing agreement, and sub-processor list). Custom questionnaires are routed to the pre-sales engineering team with a 5-business-day turnaround.

## Hand-off to account management
Once a deal closes, the prospect is introduced to an account manager within 2 business days, and onboarding is scheduled within the first week.
