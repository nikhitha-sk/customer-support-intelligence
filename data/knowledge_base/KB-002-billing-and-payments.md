# Billing, Invoices and Payment Issues

Queue: Billing and Payments

## Duplicate or incorrect charges
If a customer reports being charged twice or charged the wrong amount, first verify the transactions in the billing console using the account ID. Confirmed duplicate charges are reversed automatically within 3-5 business days; share the reversal ID with the customer. Never ask a customer to send full card numbers by e-mail; only the last four digits are needed.

## Failed payments
Payments usually fail because of an expired card, insufficient funds or a bank-side fraud block. Ask the customer to update the payment method under Settings > Billing. The account stays active during a 7-day grace period after a failed payment; after that, paid features are paused but no data is deleted.

## Invoices and tax documents
Invoices are generated on the first day of each billing cycle and can be downloaded under Settings > Billing > Invoices. To change the company name, address or VAT/GST number on future invoices, the account owner can edit the billing profile. Re-issuing past invoices requires a request to the finance team (allow 2 business days).

## Plan changes and proration
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remaining days in the cycle. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date. Annual plans cancelled mid-term are refunded on a prorated basis only within the first 30 days.

## Payment methods
We accept major credit and debit cards, UPI, bank transfer (for annual enterprise plans) and PayPal. Purchase orders are accepted for invoices above 5,000 USD.
