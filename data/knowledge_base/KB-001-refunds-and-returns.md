# Refunds, Returns and Exchanges Policy

Queue: Returns and Exchanges

## Return window
Hardware products and accessories can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided they are in original condition with all accessories. Opened software licences and digital subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of purchase if fewer than 10% of the licence seats have been activated.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or develops a defect within the 12-month warranty period, the customer is entitled to a free replacement or a full refund. Ask the customer for the order number, a short description of the defect and a photo of the damage. Customers do not pay return shipping for defective items; agents should generate a prepaid return label from the order console.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different model or size are processed as a return plus a new order. If the replacement costs more, the customer pays the difference; if it costs less, the difference is refunded to the original payment method.

## Refund timelines
Once the returned item is received and inspected (usually 2 business days), refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5-7 business days to appear; bank transfers take up to 10 business days. Agents must share the refund reference number with the customer.

## Exceptions
Items marked "final sale", gift cards and custom-configured hardware cannot be returned unless defective. Escalate any request for an exception above 500 USD to a team lead.
