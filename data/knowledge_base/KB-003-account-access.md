# Account Access, Login and Password Reset

Queue: Customer Service

## Password reset
Customers can reset their password from the sign-in page using "Forgot password". The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If the e-mail does not arrive, ask the customer to check spam folders and confirm that the address on file is correct. Agents must never set or read out a password on a customer's behalf.

## Account lockout
After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. Repeated lockouts may indicate a credential-stuffing attempt; in that case advise the customer to reset their password and enable two-factor authentication, and flag the account to the security team.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
If a customer lost access to their authenticator device, they can use one of their backup codes. Without backup codes, identity must be verified (account owner e-mail plus last invoice number) before 2FA can be reset. 2FA resets take up to 24 hours for security reasons.

## Changing account owner or e-mail
Ownership transfers require confirmation from both the current and the new owner. E-mail address changes require verification of the new address.

## Account portal outages
If the account management portal itself is unavailable, check the status page first. Customers can still use the product while the portal is down; settings changes will be possible once service is restored.
