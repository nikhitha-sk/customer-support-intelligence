# Human Resources Requests and General Inquiries

Queue: Human Resources, General Inquiry

## HR requests
Requests about payroll, leave balances, benefits enrolment and employment letters are handled by the HR operations team within 3 business days. Payroll discrepancies reported before the 20th of the month are corrected in the same month's payroll. Employment verification letters are issued within 2 business days. Sensitive matters (grievances, harassment reports) must be routed directly to an HR business partner and must not be discussed in the general support queue.

## Onboarding and offboarding
New-joiner accounts are created 3 business days before the start date once HR confirms the hire. For offboarding, access is revoked on the last working day; equipment must be returned within 7 days.

## General inquiries
For questions that do not fit another queue (partnerships, press, feedback, office information), acknowledge within 1 business day and route to the right team. Feedback and feature suggestions are logged in the product feedback tracker, and customers are thanked and told the product team reviews suggestions monthly.

## Data and privacy requests
Requests to export or delete personal data are completed within 30 days. Verify the requester's identity before processing.
